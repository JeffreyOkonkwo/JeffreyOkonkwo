# Theme tagger validation
Two random samples of tagged 2025-26 comments were read by hand (60 before tuning, 50 after).
After tuning: 45 of 50 tags fully correct (90%), 3 partly correct, 2 wrong. Estimated precision 85 to 90 percent.
Common errors: praise or abuse that mentions a keyword in passing; rigging inside party primaries rather than the general election.
Recall is lower by design: comments with no clear election wording are left untagged (1,939 of 2,230 comments were off-topic or too vague).
Breadth matters more than volume: rigging appears under 16 of 19 videos, while 43% of court comments and 80% of PVC comments sit under one video each.
