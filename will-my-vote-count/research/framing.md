# Project 2 framing: "Will My Vote Count?"

## The problem
Nigerians do not doubt that elections happen; they doubt that their vote survives the journey from the polling unit to the final declaration.
Trust in INEC sits at 34 percent (Yiaga, September 2026) and turnout fell to 27 percent in 2023 and 15 to 25 percent in recent state elections.
The presidential election is on 16 January 2027, about 14 weeks away.

## The question the project answers
Which steps in INEC's process cause the most public fear, and which fixes would rebuild the most trust before January?

## Three layers of evidence
1. **What people say.** About 10,000 public YouTube comments (January 2025 to October 2026) from at least 8 Nigerian news channels, tagged into 15 fear themes in English and Pidgin, with tone tracked by month against real events (Electoral Act vote in February 2026, primaries, campaign launch). 2023 tweets from Kaggle serve as the comparison baseline.
2. **What actually happened.** 2023 polling-unit data (CCIJ public dataset): results never uploaded (7,493), blurred uploads (12,054), by state. Off-cycle election upload speeds and turnout since 2023.
3. **How INEC works.** The process mapped step by step (registration, PVC collection, logistics, accreditation, voting, upload, collation, declaration, tribunal), each step linked to its fear themes and its evidence.

## The business analysis deliverable
A traceability matrix: fear theme to process step to evidence to proposed fix to who owns it, ranked by how much public worry each fix addresses.
Example fixes: make upload to IReV mandatory with a sanction, a public polling-unit opening time target, a PVC collection status check, publish a clean register with removed duplicates explained.

## Character
An imaginary first-time voter, based on conversations in the community spaces Jeffrey is part of. For example Amaka, 24, a graduate in Enugu with her PVC in hand, deciding whether her vote is worth the trip.

## Rules
- Nonpartisan: no candidate or party sentiment scores; the subject is the process, not the race.
- Comments anonymised: no handles, hashed IDs, only totals and short quotes published.
- Limits stated plainly: YouTube commenters lean young, urban and online; English and Pidgin only; tone, not a poll.

Research files: inec_process.md, public_fears.md, data_sources.md (same folder).
