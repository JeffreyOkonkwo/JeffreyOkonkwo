How to play
◀ ▶

Cars, keke, okada, BRTChange lane to pass them. Hitting one damages your bus, and when your hearts run out the day ends.

HOP

Potholes and speed bumpsHop over them. If you land in one, you lose speed and money for the mechanic.

DRIVE IN

Waving passengersDrive into anyone with a green marker to pick them up. They pay when you reach their stop.

TIMER

Bus stopsReach each stop before the timer runs out. If you're late, passengers pay half. Agbero collects his ticket money at every stop.

COMBO

Near misses and streaksCoins, pickups and close passes build a combo of up to x5 on your earnings. A crash resets it.

AVOID

LASTMA officerDon't drive into their lane. If they catch you, it's a ₦2,000 fine.

RISK

One-way shortcutIt saves distance, but the police chase you for 10 seconds. Crash during the chase and you pay a "settlement".

CHASE

Runaway passengerSomeone jumped off without paying. Steer into them before they escape.

HORN

Poom poom!Keke and okada in front of you move to another lane. Use it to clear the go-slow.

PARTY

Bus Jam PartyPicking up passengers fills the meter. Then music plays, you earn double naira, and traffic flies out of your way.
