## Dropped during research

- village: extra road: Village Road is a generic, unnamed setting; no single real road could be tied to it and verified, so none was added.
- douala busName 'minibus' / named minibus type: Wikipedia (Share taxi) says that as of 2008 minibuses cannot be used as share taxis in Cameroon by law; Douala's shared transport is the yellow shared taxi, so busName is 'shared taxi'. Local nicknames (clando, bend-skin) could not be verified on Wikipedia and were not used.
- Camfranglais conductor calls: No Camfranglais word could be verified on Wikipedia; plain French used instead.
- Balogun Market / Obalende road (Lagos): Verified, but only 3 Lagos roads needed; Balogun Market has no fixed address and its stops could not be confirmed street by street.
- Abuja National Mosque / National Christian Centre as landmark: Verified (Independence Avenue), but Aso Rock chosen instead to keep the road neutral; can swap in.
- Bompai Road (Kano): A road named 'Bompai Road' could not be confirmed on Wikipedia; road renamed BOMPAI · NASARAWA using verified areas.
- Sabon Gari and Sani Abacha Stadium on the Bompai road: Their exact location relative to Bompai could not be confirmed.
- Life Camp (Abuja) stop: Only indirectly confirmed as a district; left out.
- Rumuola as a Port Harcourt road name: Only confirmed as a neighbourhood bordering Trans Amadi; used as a stop there instead of its own road.
- Pedu (Cape Coast stop): No English Wikipedia article or mention found to confirm it.
- Spintex Road (Accra): Not researched in depth; three verified Accra roads chosen instead.
- Sandaga Market road (Dakar): Sandaga is already a stop on the main Dakar road; used other areas.
- Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum road (Accra): Verified, but kept Makola instead to avoid overlap with the Black Star Gate area downtown.
- Nouchi words in Abidjan calls: No Nouchi call words could be confirmed with a meaning; used French only.
- Wolof words in new Dakar calls: No additional Wolof call words verified; used French only.
- Named road 'La Beach Road', 'Palace Road', 'Elmina road': Could not confirm the exact road names; used area names on signs instead.
- Direct Wikipedia fetch: en.wikipedia.org blocked by egress proxy; all checks done via WebSearch results showing the Wikipedia articles.
- Ourika road (Marrakech): Not researched; three Marrakech roads already verified.
- MESKEL SQUARE as a Bole Road stop: Could not confirm on Wikipedia that Bole Road starts at Meskel Square.
- Nasr City and 6th of October Bridge as separate Cairo roads: Cairo limited to 3 roads; Nasr City kept as a stop on Salah Salem Road.
- OLD TAXI PARK / NEW TAXI PARK stops (Kampala): No Wikipedia article confirms them by name; Wikipedia only mentions taxi parks on the Nakasero slopes.
- Kisementi / Kololo road and Bweyogerere: Kampala limited to main plus 2 extras; not needed.
- Luganda 'kale' (okay) and other calls: Meaning not confirmed; only tugende, webale and oli otya used.
- Najjanankumbi and Zana on Entebbe Road: Not named in the Wikipedia route description.
- Kibera road (Nairobi): Neutral facts were confirmed (6.6 km from the city centre; name from Nubian 'Kibra', forest), but stops along a Kibera road could not be verified, so three better-sourced roads were used instead.
- Uhuru Park (Nairobi): Verified, but it is beside the CBD rather than on Moi Avenue; Tom Mboya Monument used as the Moi Avenue landmark instead.
- Kigali Heights and KG 9 Avenue (Kigali): No Wikipedia article for either; only passing mentions.
- Kimironko Market (Kigali): No Wikipedia article about the market; Kimironko kept only as a stop (verified sector).
- Likoni Ferry (Mombasa): Verified (free passenger ferry since 1937), but no fitting landmark kind and only two extra roads allowed; Old Town and Nyali chosen.
- Sheng word 'Tao' (town) and Kinyarwanda 'Muraho': Not independently confirmed in this pass, so not used in calls.
- Nyabugogo and Kiyovu as Kigali city-centre stops: Not confirmed on Wikipedia in this pass.
- Maboneng (Johannesburg road): No dedicated English Wikipedia article found; only passing mentions, so not enough to verify stops or a fact.
- M1 highway (Johannesburg road): Verified, but a freeway has no taxi stops; used the Nelson Mandela Bridge road for that area instead.
- Chapman's Peak Drive (Cape Town road): Verified (links Hout Bay and Noordhoek), but only two extra roads allowed; Camps Bay and Bo-Kaap chosen as better taxi routes.
- Congo River waterfront (Kinshasa road): Could not verify a named riverfront road with real stops; Gombe riverside covered by Boulevard du 30 Juin.
- Limete Tower height (210 m): One search snippet gave it, another did not confirm; left out of the fact.
- Vilakazi Street two Nobel laureates fact: Wording is 'said to be' and the exact citing article was unclear; used the Mandela House fact instead.
- Lingala kota (enter): Could not confirm meaning from a source; used yaka (come) and tokende (let us go) instead.
