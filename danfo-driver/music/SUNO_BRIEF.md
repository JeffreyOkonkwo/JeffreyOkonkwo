# Danfo Craze: Bus Jam Party music brief (for Suno)

## Background
Danfo Craze is a fast, colourful mobile runner game, made by Afrocade. You drive a yellow Lagos danfo bus
through African cities, dodging traffic, picking up passengers and escaping police. When the player grabs
the speaker power-up, a **Bus Jam Party** starts. For about 7 to 10 seconds the bus bounces, passengers dance,
confetti falls and the player earns double points. The music has to hit hard from the very first beat, feel
joyful and street-level African, and make people want to play again. The players are kids and adults, so keep
it family-friendly.

## What we need
- **20 tracks**: about 12 afrobeats and 8 amapiano (the prompts below).
- **Mostly instrumental.** A few may have short, simple vocal chants or ad-libs (like "Oya!", "Gbese!", "Jam party!").
  No full verses.
- **The best 15 to 20 seconds of each track**, cut to start right on the drop or hook, with no slow intro. Export as **MP3,
  192 kbps or higher**.
- File names: `party-01.mp3`, `party-02.mp3`, and so on.
- Loud but not clipping, with similar loudness across all tracks.

## Rights (important)
Generate these on a **paid Suno plan** (Pro or Premier). On paid plans the creator owns the songs and can use them
commercially; songs made on the free plan are for non-commercial use only. Don't reference real artists or songs in prompts,
and don't use lyrics from existing songs.

## How to use in Suno
Use **Custom** mode. Paste the **Style** text into the style box. Turn on **Instrumental** unless the prompt has a
**Lyrics** line. For prompts with lyrics, paste them in the lyrics box exactly as written.
