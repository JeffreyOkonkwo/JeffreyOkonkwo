Danfo Craze: Privacy Policy

Published by Hegenius, a business name of God Push Inc. (Toronto, Ontario, Canada) · Last updated 27 September 2026 · Covers the website and the Google Play and App Store app

Danfo Craze is a game for players aged 13 and over. You can play with no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The store app shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases. This page explains what information is used and why.

Information that stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves these things in your device's own storage. We cannot see this information:

  • your best scores, coins, missions and daily streak
  • the bus colours you unlocked and the cities you reached
  • your quiz progress (which questions you have already seen)
  • your settings: sound, language and difficulty
  • a nickname, only if you choose to type one
  • your driver's licence: the drawn driver look, flag, plate number, bus tag and conductor lines (the licence picture is drawn from options you pick; it is never a camera photo)

If you uninstall the game or clear its data, all of this is deleted.

Ads (store app only)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the menu, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for a reward. Our settings for these ads are:

  • ads are not personalised, so they are not based on your interests or on other apps you use
  • ads are limited to content rated PG or lower
  • on Android, the game does not use the advertising ID
  • on iPhone, the game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites

To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google collects information from your device. This includes your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, an app-specific identifier, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Google may share this information with its advertising partners as described in its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another place where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears before any ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time: tap your name on the game's main menu, then tap Privacy choices. The optional Remove ads purchase turns off the menu banner.

In-app purchases (store app only)

Purchases are optional and are paid through Apple or Google. The Danfo Pass is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings. Like every purchase in the game, it only changes looks and never raises a score. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which items you have bought, so that the game can unlock and restore them. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app, the items you purchased, and the store receipt. It does not receive your name or email address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not use accounts or sign-in, and we do not collect your email address, phone number or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics tools, and we do not show personalised ads.
  • The game has no chat and no private messages between players.

Brand signs in the game

Some billboards, shop signs and bus stop banners in the game show the names of brands that sponsor Danfo Craze. They are part of the scenery. No information about you is sent to these brands, and tapping them does nothing.

Online leaderboard (only if you say yes)

The leaderboard is off until you say yes. If you say yes, then after a new best run the game sends this to our game server:

  • a random player number made on your device (it is not linked to your name, email address or phone)
  • your nickname, flag, plate number, rank and drawn driver look
  • the run's score, distance, time, passengers, number of continues and level, which we use to check that the score is possible
  • whether you have the Danfo Pass, so that your row can show a gold ring

Other players can see your nickname, flag, plate, rank, driver look and score. They cannot see anything else about you. Nicknames and plates pass a word filter, and any player can report a rude name; a name that is reported several times is hidden. Our server keeps the top entries only, and older low scores are removed.

You can take yourself off at any time: open Ranks on the main menu and tap "Take me off the board". Your entry is deleted from our server straight away. The leaderboard data is stored with our hosting provider, Vercel.

City cards and trip cards

The new city card and the trip card are pictures made on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you tap Share, the picture goes only to the app you choose.

Challenging a friend

If you tap Challenge a friend, the game makes a link with your nickname and score (for example ?beat=Tunde~2500). It then opens the sharing app that you choose, such as WhatsApp or SMS. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. Once you share, the privacy policy of that app applies.

Music, fonts and game files

The game downloads its own files, such as music and quiz questions, from our game server so that it can play offline later. These downloads do not include personal information. Like any website, the server may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and device type) for a short time to keep the service running. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Age

Danfo Craze is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want information removed, email us and we will help.

Your choices and requests

  • Change ad consent: main menu, tap your name, then Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • Delete game data: uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Leave the leaderboard: main menu, Ranks, then Take me off the board.
  • Start over or remove a player: open your licence, tap Players on this phone, then Start over or Remove this player. That player's saved progress is deleted from the device and their leaderboard entry is deleted from our server. Up to 4 players can share one device, and each keeps their own saved progress.
  • Questions, or requests about information held by Google or RevenueCat for this app: email us and we will help you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version of the game is released.
