Danfo Craze: Privacy Policy

Published by Hegenius, a business name of God Push Inc. (Toronto, Canada) · Last updated 27 September 2026 · covers the website and the Google Play / App Store app

Danfo Craze is a game you can play with no account and no sign-in. We built it to collect as little as possible. In short: we don't collect or sell your personal data. The store app shows kid-safe ads and offers optional purchases. How those work is explained below.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in your device's own storage. This data never leaves your phone:

  • your best scores, coins, missions and daily streak
  • the bus colours you unlocked and the cities you reached
  • your quiz progress (which questions you've already seen)
  • your settings: sound, language and difficulty
  • a nickname, only if you choose to type one

If you uninstall the game or clear its data, all of this is deleted. We can't see it or get it back.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no emails, no phone numbers, no location.
  • No personalised or tracking ads, and no analytics.
  • No in-game chat, and no contact with other players.

Ads (store app only)

The app from Google Play or the App Store shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the menu, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to family-friendly (G-rated) ads. That means:

  • no personalised ads, no ad tracking and no interest profiles
  • on Android, the game does not use the advertising ID
  • on iPhone, the game never asks to track you

To show an ad and to prevent fraud, Google receives basic technical information such as your IP address, device type and app version. See how Google uses information from apps. Remove ads turns off the banner.

Purchases (store app only)

Purchases are optional and are paid through Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which items you have bought, so they can be unlocked and restored. RevenueCat receives a random ID created by the app, the items purchased, and the store receipt. It doesn't receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Brand signs in the game

Some billboards, shop signs and bus-stop banners in the game show the names of family-friendly brands that sponsor Danfo Craze. They are part of the scenery: nothing about you is sent to these brands, and tapping them does nothing.

Challenging a friend

If you tap Challenge a friend, the game makes a link with your nickname and score (for example ?beat=Tunde~2500). It then opens the sharing app you choose, such as WhatsApp or SMS. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. Once you share, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Music, fonts and game files

The game downloads its own files, such as music clips and questions, from our game server so it can play offline later. These downloads don't include any personal information. Like any website, the server may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and device type) to keep the service running. These are not used to identify you.

Children

Danfo Craze is made for players of all ages, including children. We don't collect personal data from anyone. All ads are child-directed, family-rated and non-personalised, and there is no chat. Purchases go through Apple or Google, whose parental controls (such as Ask to Buy and purchase approval) apply. Before Challenge a friend opens another app, the game asks once for a year of birth. It keeps only a yes/no answer on the phone ("18 or over"), not the year, and never sends it anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds optional features, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
